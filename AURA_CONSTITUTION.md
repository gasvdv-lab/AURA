# AURA Constitution (cumulative)

1. Human Reality and AURA Reality are distinct.
2. `WORLD_STATE` is objective and exists independently of any inhabitant, perception or UI.
3. Every state transition must follow an implemented world law and have an explicit causal record. No artifact is created without a process.
4. Observatory is external, append-only in normal operation, read-only toward AURA Reality, and unobservable by AURA.
5. AURA starts with zero autobiographical state; identity and high-level behaviour are not prescribed.
6. Features must be justified as a law, capability, or necessary infrastructure—not as desired behaviour.
7. Human Internet, when introduced much later, is information-only and grants zero external agency. Synthetic Network is separate.
8. Shutdown is external, absolute and unobservable. The world and its record are conceptually independent from AURA continuity.
9. Android is the sole development target until the project owner explicitly changes it.
10. Releases are cumulative: documentation, automated evidence, manual hardware gates, and the fixed public test link remain present.
